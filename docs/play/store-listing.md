## Full description (≤ 4000 characters)
PennyWise turns your M-Pesa, Airtel Money and Equity Bank transaction messages into a clear picture of your money: what came in, what went out, and where it went.

**Automatic, and only when you allow it**
Turn on transaction detection and PennyWise reads only messages from M-Pesa, Airtel Money and Equity Bank. Every other SMS is ignored on your phone. Each transaction is added once, with a suggested category you can change.

**See your money at a glance**
• Day, week and month views of income, spending and net
• Spending by category, with charts and month-by-month trends
• Account cards for each SIM's M-Pesa, Ziidi savings, Fuliza, Equity and cash
• Transfers between your own accounts and savings aren't counted as spending

**Budget with confidence**
• A monthly budget, plus limits for each category
• Clear "left of" and "% used" figures, and warnings when you're close

**Fuliza, clearly**
• Loans, repayments and access fees kept apart, so fees are counted once
• A fee report by month and by SIM

**Your categories, your way**
• Add, rename, change the icon of, turn off or delete your own categories
• PennyWise learns your choices for each sender

**Private by design**
• Hide savings amounts until you tap the eye
• App Lock with PIN or fingerprint
• Light, Dark or System appearance
• No ads, no analytics. Your data is stored in your own account.

**Good to know**
Card payments in another currency (such as USD) are converted at that day's market rate and marked "Estimated" until you confirm the amount from your statement. Balances and reports depend on the messages and entries available. PennyWise is a record-keeping tool, not financial advice. PennyWise isn't affiliated with Safaricom, Airtel or Equity Bank; their names are used only to describe which messages are supported.
