Delete your PennyWise account

This page deletes your PennyWise account and all its data. You can also do it in the app: Settings › Delete account.

Deletion is permanent and can't be undone.

It deletes:

  • Your sign-in account
  • All your transactions, including the masked copies of transaction SMS
  • Your accounts, starting balances and balance history
  • Budgets, category limits, your own categories and learned categories
  • Your profile and profile photo

PennyWise keeps no other copies of your data and nothing is kept for legal reasons. Google, which hosts PennyWise's database, may keep encrypted system backups and logs for a limited time under its own policies. SMS on your phone and your Google account itself aren't affected. To also clear PennyWise's settings on your phone, uninstall the app.
